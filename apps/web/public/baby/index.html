Common questions

What does it cost?

Your first photo is free. After that, one photo is ₹149. A full shoot is ₹699 for 6 photos, ₹1,099 for 10 or ₹1,499 for 15. Prices include GST, and you see the price before you pay. There is nothing else to buy.

What if I don't like a photo?

Every photo comes with one free remake: same set, a fresh take. If a photo fails to make, you are not charged for it.

Can I get a refund?

If something went wrong, message us within 7 days of your shoot. We'll fix it or refund you.

How many photos of my baby should I add?

One clear photo is enough. Three to five from different angles give the best likeness. Good light, face visible, no filters.

Which ages does it work for?

Our sets are made for newborns, from birth to about 10 weeks. Older babies still work, but results are best in that window.

What happens to my baby's photos?

They are used only to make your pictures. They go securely to our image partner for that, and to no one else. We never sell them or use them for ads. Delete them, or your whole account, from You at any time. Our image partner may hold a copy for up to 30 days for safety checks before it is erased.

What sizes do I get?

Each photo is made in the shape you choose: Post (4:5), Square (1:1) or Story (9:16). Every photo is full quality with no watermark, ready to share or print at standard photo sizes.
