# Evidence-led photography direction — 1 November 2026

The 90 supplied references were reviewed for product logic, composition, lighting, material response, human direction and production boundaries. The individual audit stays local; source images are not published in the app, used to imitate a person, or attached to routine provider calls.

The code review found that the final renderer omitted utility and ingredient intelligence, shot plans carried no structured product rationale, and generic photographic booleans did not record craft evidence. A separate source-to-prompt pipeline was not required: the existing cached analysis and one coordinated text-planning call can carry this information without paid concept previews or automatic image retries.

The new contract adds a bounded, provenance-aware product fact ledger. Label-based flavours and ingredients must quote readable text and name the actual substance. Vitamin C does not prove citrus; amber glass does not prove honey; pineapple fragrance does not turn lip balm into food. Visual construction, materials, utility, buyer context and uncertainties travel with the same analysis.

Every new non-Studio shot plan contains an idea, cited product facts, photographic mechanism, product role, light design, camera/focus appearance, styling, gesture, finish and evidenced ingredient props. Fifteen compatible photographic principles cover graphic shadow, material tonality, colour, reflection, facets, ingredient geometry, frozen material motion, detail, editorial gesture, human detail, drape, architectural depth, useful action, near/far hierarchy and authored still life. These are mechanisms, not fixed backdrops or a promise of infinite unique art. Category, human, crop and pure-white constraints filter them.

The renderer receives the fact ledger and full photographic treatment, plus material-specific finish and human-contact instructions. The existing review call records concrete light/material/optical/human/ingredient observations. Specific visible defects can block delivery; uncertain taste and incidental planner props do not become spurious failures. Concept observations are advisory. An AI judge is not proof of world-class taste.

The prior cost reservations and one-image-per-attempt profile remain. No additional paid image call, reference-preview generation, previous-output external comparison, or silent quality/model upgrade is introduced. Larger exports are still resizes of the native render; a larger pixel count is not newly captured detail.

Some supplied examples visibly contain AI markers, credits, mockup wording, deliberate suspension, apparent product multiplication or hybrid branding. Their visual appeal is not evidence of genuine formulation, provenance or feasible hidden construction. Exact camera/lens/light hardware cannot be established from pixels. Learn the decisions and create an original composition while preserving actual merchandise.

Current official implementation guidance: [OpenAI image prompting](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-prompting). Camera specifications guide appearance rather than guaranteeing a physical simulation. Real output comparisons, source fidelity, first-pass acceptance and total cost per usable image remain the release evidence.
